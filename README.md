# Midnight Monsters

A pixel-art arcade racer with 16 monster drivers, four haunted locations and races for two to four players online or in local split-screen.

**[Play Midnight Monsters in your browser](https://cyrilbele.github.io/midnight_monsters/)**

## Race with friends

[Open the online lobby](https://cyrilbele.github.io/midnight_monsters/?online=1), choose 2, 3 or 4 players, create a room and share its invitation link. Once everyone has joined, the host chooses **Start race**. Enable your microphone to talk or choose **Listen to voices** to listen. During the race, press **V** or **Y / △** on a gamepad to toggle your microphone. Keep the host’s tab open throughout the race.

## Run locally

From the project directory, run `python3 -m http.server 8642 --bind 127.0.0.1` and open [the game](http://localhost:8642/).

GitHub Pages publishes the game from the root of `main`. For track editing, translations and sprite sheets, see the [development guide](AGENTS.md).
