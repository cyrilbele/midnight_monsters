Each press spends one of your two turbo charges. Pick up a green potion or make a close pass in traffic to regain one. You can also drive with WASD or ZQSD.

Touchscreen

Hold the left or right side to accelerate and steer. Tap the turbo gauge with another finger to boost.

Garage

Use left and right to choose a ride and up and down to rotate it. Press Enter to race or Esc to return to the title screen.
